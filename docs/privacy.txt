Privacy Policy for JULIENNE PIERRE-LOUIS

Effective date: May 12, 2026

JULIENNE PIERRE-LOUIS is provided by JULIENNE PIERRE-LOUIS. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app lets you save operations records, payroll report notes, shift assignments, facility reservations, training progress, and app preferences. This information is stored on your device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
JULIENNE PIERRE-LOUIS does not require account registration. It does not upload operations records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share your operations records or preferences with JULIENNE PIERRE-LOUIS or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operations records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.
